## workshop

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a premium editorial 3D photographic artwork for the hero of a personal creative web developer portfolio named Taller (workshop). Landscape composition approximately 3:2. This is a standalone visual asset, absolutely NO typography, NO letters, NO logos, NO watermark, NO website screenshot. Art direction: tactile surrealism meets sophisticated contemporary industrial design. A sculptural playful desktop computer made from unglazed warm ivory ceramic and burnt vermilion orange metal on an architectural plinth. The computer screen is a beautiful deep forest green glass with a simple abstract luminous cream geometric loop, not a UI. Beside it, a sculptural orange coiled cable, one floating ivory folded-paper form, a small brushed-aluminium sphere, a moss-green block and a very small chrome cursor-like arrow as a sculpture. A subtle large circular cream disc in the background ties the objects together like an exploded designer's tool kit. It must feel like a tangible still life about building digital tools from ideas, not a generic desk with laptop. Warm butter yellow seamless studio background (#e9e4c9), hard gorgeous late afternoon sunlight from upper left, long bold orange-tinted shadows, beautiful film grain, realistic material imperfections, crisp textures and excellent studio art direction. Palette limited to ivory, butter yellow, forest green, terracotta orange and brushed silver. Camera three-quarter angle, 50mm product photography lens. Compose main objects in centre with room around edges for responsive cropping, full scene visible, substantial objects filling 75% of frame. Ambitious award-winning creative studio quality, minimalist but visually striking and playful.

## orbit

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a cinematic premium 3D artwork for a futuristic personal developer portfolio called Orbit. Landscape approximately 16:10. NO text, NO letters, NO logos, NO watermark, NO interface screenshot. A monumental beautiful alien computational sculpture in deep space: a huge black chrome sphere whose surface has fine concentric etched circuit grooves and a glowing mint glass inset, floating through a thick broad twisted metallic orbital ribbon. The ribbon should be an extraordinary continuous sculptural loop of brushed titanium and pearlescent violet glass, with some smooth lilac ceramic panels, realistic reflections. Two small satellite spheres of frosted mint glass and iridescent violet float nearby with elegant thin orbital light trails. Under the sculpture is a soft teal and violet atmospheric mist against almost-black midnight navy (#080e18) with sparse pin-sharp distant stars. Camera is close enough to appreciate tactile surface detail, main sculpture placed just right of centre filling 70% of image, fully visible within frame including the entire rings. Dramatic rim lighting, mint on left, ultraviolet right; realistic path-traced rendering, subtle film grain, extreme sophistication. A sense of exploration, curiosity and technological craft. No astronaut, no spaceship, no planets with continents, no stock sci-fi explosion or busy nebula. Negative dark breathing room around the object, especially upper left. Make this a gallery-worthy abstract science-fiction sculpture photographed like a luxury industrial design object, visually arresting and clearly dimensional.
